What happened

Primary-source checked

The Forum today

The Parliament of India hosts the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum on 26-27 Oct 2026 under India's 2026 BRICS Presidency; the theme is 'Strengthening Parliaments for People-Centric Development' (PIB).

Who sits at the table

BRICS has 11 full members: the original five (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa), the 2024 intake (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE) and Indonesia (January 2025). A separate partner ring of ten countries was created at the 2024 Kazan Summit. The Forum brings together parliamentary delegations, including from Cuba, Egypt and Belarus (PIB; membership lists from the official presidency site).

Sessions and protocol

The release lists four sessions - Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability - plus a meeting of chairpersons of International Affairs Committees. The Prime Minister is to grace the event and the Vice-President is to host a banquet, as announced in the release.

The bilateral track

Ahead of the Forum, the Speaker held bilateral meetings with delegations including Russia (State Duma Chairman Volodin), Cuba, Egypt and Belarus. The release notes that India and Russia mark 80 years of diplomatic relations in 2027, and carries the Speaker's appreciation of Russia's support for India's permanent UNSC seat.

The hub: one parliamentary forum ties the BRICS expansion, legislative diplomacy and the India-Russia track to the 2026 Presidency.

With reference to the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum, consider the following statements:

  1. It is being hosted by the Parliament of India on 26-27 October 2026.
  2. Its theme is 'Strengthening Parliaments for People-Centric Development'.
  3. Indonesia is the most recent country to join BRICS as a full member.
  4. The Forum is an annual summit of BRICS heads of state or government.

Answer: (b) 1, 2 and 3 only

(b) is correct: the Parliament of India hosts the Forum on 26-27 October 2026 and the theme is ‘Strengthening Parliaments for People-Centric Development’ (PIB); Indonesia is the most recent full member of BRICS, having joined in January 2025 (official BRICS 2026 presidency site).

(a) misses true statement 3: Indonesia’s full membership (January 2025) is the most recent, after the January 2024 intake.

(c) includes false statement 4: this is a forum of parliamentary delegations, not the annual summit of BRICS heads of state or government.

(d) includes false statement 4 for the same reason. Sources: PIB (Lok Sabha Secretariat) release of 25 October 2026; membership detail from the official BRICS 2026 presidency site (check).
