The 24th instalment

The 24th instalment of PM-KISAN was released on 30 Oct 2026 at Banaskantha, Gujarat: more than Rs.18,690 crore moved directly to over 9.34 crore eligible farmer families, taking cumulative support past Rs.4.64 lakh crore (PIB).

The scheme

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi), launched 24 Feb 2019, gives eligible landholding farmer families Rs.6,000 a year in three equal Rs.2,000 instalments, paid into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts through Direct Benefit Transfer. It is a Central Sector Scheme - 100% funded by the Government of India - while States and UTs identify and verify eligible families (guidelines; pmkisan.gov.in).

Who qualifies, who is excluded

The benefit unit is the farmer family: husband, wife and minor children owning cultivable land as per land records. Excluded: institutional landholders; income-tax payers in the last assessment year; registered practising professionals; government employees above Class IV/MTS level; pensioners drawing Rs.10,000 or more a month (with a named exception); constitutional-post holders and elected representatives (guidelines).

The road ahead

In July 2026 the Union Cabinet approved the scheme's continuation from 2026-27 to 2030-31 with an outlay of Rs.3.15 lakh crore (PIB). e-KYC is mandatory, and the portal's 'Know Your Status' lets a farmer see where the payment chain stands (pmkisan.gov.in).

The hub: one instalment at Banaskantha ties the Central Sector design, the family rule, the exclusions, the DBT chain and the land records underneath - who gets support, on what conditions, and how the money travels.

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN), consider the following statements:

  1. It is a Central Sector Scheme, fully funded by the Government of India.
  2. The annual benefit of Rs.6,000 is paid separately to each adult member of an eligible farmer family.
  3. Its 24th instalment was released at Banaskantha in Gujarat.
  4. e-KYC is optional for beneficiaries under the scheme.

Answer: (a) 1 and 3 only

(a) is correct: PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme fully funded by the Government of India, and its 24th instalment was released at Banaskantha, Gujarat, on 30 October 2026 (guidelines; PIB).

(b) includes false statement 2: the Rs.6,000 annual benefit is per eligible farmer family, not per adult member (guidelines).

(c) includes false statements 2 and 4: the benefit is per family, and e-KYC is mandatory, not optional (pmkisan.gov.in).

(d) includes false statement 4 for the same reason. Sources: revised PM-KISAN operational guidelines; pmkisan.gov.in; PIB releases of 30 October 2026.
