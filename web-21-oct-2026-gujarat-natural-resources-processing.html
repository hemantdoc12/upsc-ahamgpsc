What happened

Press-reported

The visit

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio is in India on 20-22 Oct 2026 on an energy-focused trip. He met External Affairs Minister Jaishankar in Mumbai on 20 Oct and is due to tour Reliance's Jamnagar refinery complex in Gujarat; the US ambassador called the trip business-focused (press-reported).

The complex

Jamnagar, near the Gulf of Kutch, is the world's largest single-site refining complex: about 1.4 million barrels a day, roughly 1.5% of global refining capacity (company-reported). Its two refineries were commissioned in 1999 and 2008, the second in a special economic zone, and it has processed more than 216 crude grades (company-reported).

The crude politics

Rubio said he would talk with Reliance about refining more Venezuelan crude; Kpler estimates Reliance took 350,000-370,000 barrels a day of the October intake. The backdrop: US pressure on buyers of Russian energy, the war on Iran and American diesel prices (press-reported).

The Gujarat frame

The stop puts a processing economy in the frame: the country's longest coastline at 2,340.62 km on the new NHO-Survey of India measure (PIB), about three-fourths of India's salt from the Rann, Surat polishing about nine in ten of the world's diamonds under 50% US tariffs, the Cambay basin's old fields, a 30 GW renewable park at Khavda, and the Alang ship-breaking yard (press-reported and reference figures - check details).

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to the geography of Gujarat, consider the following statements:

  1. Gujarat has the longest coastline among all Indian states.
  2. India's first Marine National Park lies in the Gulf of Kutch.
  3. The Khavda hybrid renewable energy park is coming up in the Kutch district.
  4. The Jamnagar refining complex is located on the Gulf of Khambhat.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Answer: (b) 1, 2 and 3 only

(a) 1 and 2 only. Fails by dropping statement 3, which is true: the Khavda park is in Kutch district. Mistake tested: knowing the headline capacity but not the location.

(b) 1, 2 and 3 only. Correct. Statement 1 holds on the re-assessed measure (2,340.62 km, the longest of any state); statement 2 is standard reference knowledge; statement 3 matches project reporting.

(c) 2, 3 and 4 only. Fails on statement 4, which is false: Jamnagar sits by the Gulf of Kutch, not the Gulf of Khambhat. Mistake tested: the two gulfs - the classic Gujarat mix-up.

(d) 1, 2, 3 and 4. Fails on the same false statement 4. Mistake tested: the same gulf confusion, one option up.
