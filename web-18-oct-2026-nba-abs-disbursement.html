NBA disburses Rs 1.19 crore of ABS money

NBA disburses Rs 1.19 crore of Access and Benefit Sharing money to 29 State Biodiversity Boards and one UT council, announced 17 Oct 2026

The headline is a small number with a big idea: companies paid for using farm genetic resources, and the money now travels to State boards by a formula. Behind it sit a 2002 Act, a 2010 protocol, a statistics portal, and the plain fact that an allocation method is a policy choice, not a natural law. Each angle below is a doorway, not a question.

What happened

Press-reported

The disbursement

On 17 Oct 2026 the National Biodiversity Authority announced it has disbursed Rs 1.19 crore (Rs 1,18,61,655) under the Access and Benefit Sharing mechanism to 29 State Biodiversity Boards and one Union Territory Biodiversity Council (PIB).

Where the money came from

Companies paid ABS contributions for research and commercial utilisation of agricultural biological resources: maize, rice, pearl millet and cotton. PIB names Corteva Crop India, Pioneer Overseas Corporation, Pioneer Hi-Bred, Limagrain India (formerly Bisco Biosciences), Limagrain (Thailand), Limagrain Brasil, Syngenta India and Crystal Crop Protection.

How it was divided

Because the resources were procured through markets or traders, individual benefit claimers could not be identified. The NBA used an Expert Committee method: allocations proportional to estimated cultivation areas of the crops, from the Agriculture Ministry's Unified Portal for Agricultural Statistics. Maharashtra leads with Rs 19,46,149; 'Others' accounts for Rs 21,86,843 (PIB).

Conditions and context

Boards must use the funds exclusively for Section 32(2) activities: biodiversity documentation, conservation and sustainable use, strengthening local biodiversity governance, research, capacity building and community livelihoods. PIB notes the NBA has disbursed Rs 86 crore from the agriculture sector and over Rs 203 crore in total, and links the announcement to CBD COP17 in Yerevan (19-30 Oct 2026).

The hub, in one line Reported: NBA disbursed Rs 1.19 crore of company-paid ABS money to State biodiversity boards, divided by crop area.

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to the National Biodiversity Authority's recent disbursement, consider the following statements:

  1. The NBA disbursed Rs 1.19 crore under Access and Benefit Sharing to 29 State Biodiversity Boards and one UT Biodiversity Council.
  2. The funds were distributed equally among all States and Union Territories.
  3. The biological resources involved include maize, rice, pearl millet and cotton.
  4. The Boards may use the disbursed funds for any of their administrative expenses.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Answer: (a) 1 and 3 only

(a) 1 and 3 only. Correct. Statement 1 is the PIB headline fact. Statement 3 is the PIB crop list (maize, rice, pearl millet, cotton).

(b) 1, 2 and 3 only. Fails on statement 2, which is false: allocation was proportional to estimated cultivation area, not equal. Mistake tested: assuming equal shares in a federal transfer.

(c) 1, 3 and 4 only. Fails on statement 4, which is false: funds are restricted to Section 32(2) activities. Mistake tested: treating earmarked funds as general revenue.

(d) 2 and 4 only. Fails on both statements 2 and 4, and drops the two true ones. Mistake tested: the same two errors.
