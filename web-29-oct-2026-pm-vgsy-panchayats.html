What happened

Primary-source checked

The new scheme

The Union Cabinet has approved PM VGSY, a Centrally Sponsored Scheme with an outlay of Rs.7,395.55 crore, to build planning, audit and revenue capacity across 2.55 lakh+ Panchayats as the Sixteenth Finance Commission recommends Rs.4.35 lakh crore in grants for Rural Local Bodies (PIB, 28 Oct 2026).

The money

Total outlay Rs.7,395.55 crore over five years: Central share Rs.4,869.48 crore, State share Rs.2,526.07 crore. It sits alongside the Sixteenth Finance Commission's recommendation of about Rs.4.35 lakh crore in grants for Rural Local Bodies for 2026-27 to 2030-31 (PIB).

What it builds

Training for about 32 lakh elected representatives and functionaries; contractual support staff at State, District and Block levels, with no permanent posts; Gram Panchayat Development Plans on eGramSwaraj; at least 2.55 lakh audit plans a year on AuditOnline; the PANCHAM learning platform in 17 languages; and support for Panchayats' Own Source Revenue (PIB).

How it runs

The scheme covers all States and UTs, including traditional local bodies in areas where Part IX does not apply. Implementation runs through States and UTs and their Annual Action Plans, with a designated Central Empowered Committee approving and reviewing them (PIB).

The hub: one capacity-building scheme ties Part IX, Finance Commission money, eGramSwaraj plumbing and the question of whether money makes local government stronger.

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM VGSY), consider the following statements:

  1. It is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme approved for implementation from 2026-27 to 2030-31.
  2. It provides for the creation of permanent posts at the Block level.
  3. It supports annual preparation of Gram Panchayat Development Plans on the eGramSwaraj portal.
  4. It is limited to areas where Part IX of the Constitution applies.

Answer: (a) 1 and 3 only

(a) is correct: PM VGSY is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme approved for implementation from 2026-27 to 2030-31, and it supports annual preparation of Gram Panchayat Development Plans on the eGramSwaraj portal (PIB, 28 Oct 2026).

(b) includes false statement 2: the scheme creates no permanent posts; staffing is contractual at State, District and Block levels (PIB).

(c) includes false statements 2 and 4: there are no permanent posts, and the scheme covers all States and UTs including traditional local bodies in areas where Part IX does not apply (PIB).

(d) includes false statement 4 for the same reason. Source: PIB release of 28 October 2026.
