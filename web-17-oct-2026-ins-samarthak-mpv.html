INS Samarthak, first MPV and first private-yard warship

INS Samarthak, the Navy's first Multi-Purpose Vessel and a private yard's first major warship, commissioned at Karwar on 16 Oct 2026

The headline is a first: a major warship from a private Indian shipyard. Behind it sit a new class of utility vessel, a west-coast base, an indigenisation policy, an unmanned-systems future, and the plain fact that PIB-stated roles are claims, not test results. Each angle below is a doorway, not a question.

What happened

Press-reported

The commissioning

On 16 Oct 2026 INS Samarthak, the first of class of the Multi-Purpose Vessels, was commissioned into the Indian Navy at Naval Base Karwar, Karnataka. The Vice-President, Shri C. P. Radhakrishnan, presided; the Chief of the Naval Staff, Adm Krishna Swaminathan, was present (PIB).

The ship

Designed and constructed by Larsen & Toubro, Samarthak is described by PIB as the first major warship built by an Indian private shipyard, with over 75% indigenous content and extensive modular construction and pre-outfitting. PIB's 13 Oct curtain-raiser says it is the first of two MPVs being inducted. The name means 'The Proponent'; the crest shows an elephant emerging from waves; the motto is 'Victory is Our Sole Aim'.

Stated roles

PIB lists maritime surveillance and reconnaissance, offshore patrolling, humanitarian assistance and disaster relief, low-intensity maritime operations, target deployment and trials, service as an unmanned/autonomous system mothership, conversion to a limited hospital ship and salvage operations. The Vice-President said it will support trials of next-generation weapons, sensors and unmanned systems.

What is a claim

Capabilities and roles are as stated by PIB and were not independently tested. The Vice-President's remarks on heritage and on the Navy's Indian Ocean role are his statements as reported. This page takes no side on them.

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to INS Samarthak, consider the following statements:

  1. INS Samarthak is the Indian Navy's first Multi-Purpose Vessel, designed and constructed by an Indian private shipyard.
  2. It was commissioned at Naval Base Karwar, which lies on the coast of Kerala.
  3. The ship has over 75% indigenous content and uses modular construction with pre-outfitting.
  4. It is built solely for combat duties and cannot support humanitarian assistance and disaster relief.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Answer: (a) 1 and 3 only

(a) 1 and 3 only. Correct. Statement 1 is PIB: first of class MPV, designed and constructed by Larsen & Toubro, the first major warship from an Indian private shipyard. Statement 3 is PIB: over 75% indigenous content, modular construction and pre-outfitting.

(b) 1, 2 and 3 only. Fails on statement 2, which is false: Karwar is in Karnataka, not Kerala. Mistake tested: the west-coast map trap.

(c) 1, 3 and 4 only. Fails on statement 4, which is false: HADR is one of the stated roles, alongside surveillance, trials and unmanned-system support. Mistake tested: assuming a warship is single-role.

(d) 2 and 4 only. Fails on both statements 2 and 4, and drops the two true ones. Mistake tested: the same geography and capability errors.
