What happened

Primary-source checked

The pathway

UNEP’s Emissions Gap Report 2026: The Point of Return sets out a lowest-possible overshoot pathway: a 1.7-1.8°C peak around mid-century, with about a 50% chance of returning to or below 1.5°C by 2100. Deep cuts and CO2 removal are both needed.

The cuts

Global GHG cuts of 18% by 2030, 45% by 2035 and 69% by 2040 are relative to 2025. Full NDC delivery yields only 5-8% by 2030 and 11-15% by 2035 on that same baseline. UNEP gives a 5.7% average annual reduction rate for 2025-2035.

The three futures

Continued current policies could bring 2.8°C warming this century and further warming thereafter. All NDCs, with similar efforts continued, imply 2.6°C; adding all net-zero targets gives 2.1°C. These require implementation, not merely announcements.

The removal bill

The lowest-overshoot pathway needs roughly 320 Gt CO2 removed from the atmosphere and permanently stored over the century. UNEP flags socioeconomic and technological uncertainty. Every five more years of CO2 emissions at current levels adds around 0.1°C to peak warming in this assessment.

The hub: a return to 1.5°C needs near-term cuts, implemented policies and durable CO2 removal, not a more distant promise.

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to UNEP’s Emissions Gap Report 2026, consider the following statements:

  1. The 18%, 45% and 69% emissions cuts use 2025 as their baseline.
  2. Full delivery of all NDCs alone limits warming to 1.5°C this century.
  3. The lowest-possible overshoot pathway needs CO2 removal as well as emissions cuts.
  4. A return to or below 1.5°C by 2100 is guaranteed.

Answer: (b) 1 and 3 only

(a) omits true statement 3 and includes false statement 2: NDC delivery implies 2.6°C, not 1.5°C.

(b) is correct: 2025 is the baseline, and the pathway needs cuts plus CO2 removal.

(c) omits true statement 1 and includes false statements 2 and 4. A return is conditional with about a 50% chance, not guaranteed.

(d) includes the same false statements 2 and 4. Source: UNEP’s official release.

Honesty note

  • Primary sources: Report figures checked against UNEP’s 21 Oct release and official report record.
  • Scenario, not promise: The return by 2100 is conditional, with about a 50% chance in the pathway.
  • Baseline matters: 18%, 45% and 69% cuts are relative to 2025. They are not national quotas.
  • Limits: This is a source-based study map, not a technical audit of every model assumption.
