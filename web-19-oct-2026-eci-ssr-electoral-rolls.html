ECI orders Special Summary Revision of voter lists

ECI orders a Special Summary Revision of electoral rolls in 17 States and UTs; qualifying date 1 January 2027, final rolls 4 January 2027

The headline is an administrative order with a constitutional spine: who gets onto the voter list, and who guards the gates. Behind it sit Article 324, adult suffrage, a 1950 Act, a timetable of forms, and the quiet fact that a roll revision is where the franchise is actually made. Each angle below is a doorway, not a question.

What happened

Press-reported

The order

On 15 Oct 2026 the Election Commission of India ordered (No. 23/2026-ERS) a Special Summary Revision of electoral rolls in 17 States and UTs - from Assam and Bihar to Tamil Nadu, UP and West Bengal - with 1 January 2027 as the qualifying date, under Section 21 of the RP Act, 1950 (press-reported).

The schedule

Polling stations are rationalised by 2 November, the draft roll publishes on 12 November, claims and objections run to 11 December, disposal by 28 December, and the final roll publishes on 4 January 2027. Voters turning eighteen on 1 April, 1 July or 1 October 2027 can file advance applications (press-reported).

SSR versus SIR

A summary revision updates the existing roll incrementally, without house-to-house visits; the Special Intensive Revision rebuilds the roll from scratch. This is the first SSR since the January 2025 round - the annual exercise was disrupted while the SIR ran (press-reported).

The safeguards

EROs must personally cross-verify deletions above 2% of a polling station's electors, or any objector behind more than five cases; a 9 October order caps bulk Form 7 filings. No polling station may exceed about 1,200 electors without ECI approval (press-reported).

The hub, in one line Reported: ECI orders the annual voter-list revision in 17 States and UTs, with final rolls on 4 January 2027.

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to the Special Summary Revision (SSR) of electoral rolls, consider the following statements:

  1. The Special Summary Revision is conducted under Section 21 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950.
  2. Booth Level Officers carry out house-to-house verification of electors during a summary revision.
  3. Those turning eighteen on 1 April, 1 July or 1 October 2027 can file advance enrolment applications in this SSR.
  4. The final electoral rolls under this revision are due on 1 January 2027.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Answer: (a) 1 and 3 only

(a) 1 and 3 only. Correct. Statement 1 is the reported basis of the order. Statement 3 matches the reported advance-application window for the three later 2027 qualifying dates.

(b) 1, 2 and 3 only. Fails on statement 2, which is false: house-to-house verification belongs to an intensive revision, not a summary one. Mistake tested: mixing up SSR and SIR.

(c) 1, 3 and 4 only. Fails on statement 4, which is false: final rolls are due on 4 January 2027; 1 January 2027 is the qualifying date. Mistake tested: confusing the qualifying date with the publication date.

(d) 2 and 4 only. Fails on both, and drops the two true statements. Mistake tested: the same two errors.
