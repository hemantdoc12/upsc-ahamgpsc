8 Nov 2026 · E20 petrol and ethanol blending Economy

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol blending in petrol in India has increased from less than 1.5% in 2013-14 to 20% in 2025-26.
  2. India achieved its 20% blending target five years ahead of the schedule originally set for 2030.
  3. India's ethanol production capacity in 2026 is lower than it was in 2014.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: blending rose from under 1.5% (2013-14) to 20% (2025-26), and the 20% target (originally for 2030, later advanced to 2025-26) was met five years ahead of the original schedule. Statement 3 is wrong: capacity grew nearly fivefold, from 421 crore litres (2014) to about 2,000 crore litres (2026). [PIB Factsheet, 5 Jul 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG factsheet, 5 Jul 2026)

Question 2

With reference to ethanol production for the EBP Programme, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol is procured from sugarcane-based sources as well as grain-based sources such as maize and surplus rice.
  2. Only surplus rice, as determined and permitted by the Department of Food and Public Distribution after meeting national food security requirements, is diverted for ethanol production.
  3. Second-generation (2G) ethanol projects use lignocellulosic biomass such as agricultural residue as feedstock.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: D

All three are correct. The 1G supply base is sugarcane juice/molasses, maize and surplus rice; the DFPD permits only surplus rice after food-security needs are met; and 2G plants (supported under PM JI-VAN Yojana, Rs 1,969.50 crore for 12 projects, 2018-24) use lignocellulosic residue. [PIB Factsheet 5 Jul 2026; MoPNG 2G page]

Source: PIB (MoPNG factsheet, 5 Jul 2026) + MoPNG 2G ethanol page

Question 3

With reference to the fuel properties of ethanol and E20 petrol, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol has a research octane number (RON) of about 108.5, higher than that of petrol (about 84.4).
  2. Blending ethanol to E20 raises the effective octane rating of Indian petrol to around 95.
  3. Ethanol has a higher calorific value per litre than petrol, which increases the energy content of the blend.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct (RON ~108.5 vs ~84.4; E20 lifts effective octane to ~95). Statement 3 is wrong: ethanol's calorific value is about 30% LOWER than petrol's - the source of the mileage debate, though real-world service data (Maruti Suzuki) puts the drop at only ~0.6 km/l for a 20 km/l car. [PIB Factsheet, 5 Jul 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG factsheet, 5 Jul 2026)

Question 4

With reference to IS 17021, the BIS specification for ethanol-blended motor gasoline, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol content may be determined by Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) as per ASTM D5845.
  2. When Gas Chromatography under ASTM D4815/D6839 is used, the sample must first be diluted 1:1 with oxygenate-free petrol and the result corrected against an E10 certified reference material.
  3. Any private laboratory certificate mentioning the ethanol percentage is sufficient to establish non-conformity of a fuel sample under this standard.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 state the prescribed IS 17021:2018 (Amendment 3, Aug 2025) methods. Statement 3 is wrong: conformity must be established through the prescribed methods, with sampling under the MS/HSD Order 2005 and MDG 2024 (six one-litre samples, jointly signed labels, Schedule III laboratories) - a bare third-party certificate is a claim, not a finding. [PIB/MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026)

Question 5

With reference to the OMC/CHT Quality Coordination Cell (QCC) exercise reported in November 2026, consider the following statements:

  1. 893 E20 samples drawn from IOCL, BPCL and HPCL depots and retail outlets across 27 States and UTs were found within the prescribed 19-21% ethanol content range.
  2. All 56 samples tested in Karnataka recorded ethanol content between 19% and 20%.
  3. The exercise establishes that every retail outlet in India supplies fuel conforming to specifications.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are the reported QCC results (893 samples within 19-21%; Karnataka's 56 at 19-20%, average 19.30%). Statement 3 overreaches: a sampling exercise cannot certify every outlet, and the OMCs' own request for joint sampling and independent testing of the disputed Karnataka findings (26-31% claim) remains pending. [PIB/MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026)

7 Nov 2026 · Semicon 2.0, Sanand and Dholera Science

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to Semicon India 2.0, consider the following statements:

  1. The web application portal launched on 6 November 2026 is open for applications under Pillars 2, 3 and 4.
  2. The programme is structured around six pillars, including Design, Research and Development, and Talent.
  3. The portal invites applications under all six pillars simultaneously.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: the 6 November portal accepts applications for Pillars 2 (Machines and Materials), 3 (More Fabs) and 4 (ATMP/OSAT strengthening), and the programme has six pillars including Design, R&D and Talent. Statement 3 is wrong: Pillars 1, 5 and 6 are not part of this application window.

Source: PIB (MeitY, 6 Nov 2026) + Micron (28 Feb 2026) + Tata Electronics

Question 2

With reference to semiconductor facilities in Gujarat, consider the following pairs:

  1. Sanand: Micron - assembly, testing, marking and packaging (ATMP) facility
  2. Dholera: Tata Electronics - wafer fabrication plant in partnership with PSMC of Taiwan
  3. Dholera: Tata Electronics - India's first operational outsourced semiconductor assembly and test (OSAT) facility

Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?

Correct answer: C

Pairs 1 and 2 are correctly matched: Micron's Sanand plant is an assembly and test (ATMP) facility, and Tata Electronics' Dholera plant is a wafer fab being built with PSMC of Taiwan. Pair 3 is wrong: the Dholera project is a front-end fab, not an OSAT unit, and it is still under construction.

Source: PIB (MeitY, 6 Nov 2026) + Micron (28 Feb 2026) + Tata Electronics

Question 3

With reference to Micron's semiconductor facility at Sanand, consider the following statements:

  1. It is described as India's first semiconductor assembly and test facility.
  2. It converts DRAM and NAND wafers from Micron's global manufacturing network into finished memory and storage products.
  3. It is yet to begin commercial production and holds an ISO 14001 environmental certification.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: D

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: Micron calls Sanand India's first assembly and test facility of its kind, and it converts DRAM and NAND wafers into finished products. Statement 3 is wrong: the site has already begun commercial production and its certification is ISO 9001:2015 (quality), not ISO 14001.

Source: PIB (MeitY, 6 Nov 2026) + Micron (28 Feb 2026) + Tata Electronics

Question 4

With reference to the Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS), consider the following statements:

  1. The latest approvals take the scheme's total to 118 applications covering products across 16 states.
  2. Copper foil, cathode active material and specialty ceramics have received approvals under ECMS for the first time.
  3. The 12 newly approved applications carry a projected investment of about Rs 77,051 crore.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: ECMS now totals 118 approved applications across 16 states, and copper foil, cathode active material and specialty ceramics are first-time approvals. Statement 3 is wrong: the 12 new approvals carry projected investment of Rs 7,503 crore; Rs 77,051 crore is the cumulative figure for all 118 applications.

Source: PIB (MeitY, 6 Nov 2026) + Micron (28 Feb 2026) + Tata Electronics

Question 5

With reference to the Chips to Startup (C2S) programme, consider the following statements:

  1. It has provided electronic design automation (EDA) tools to more than 300 academic institutions.
  2. It has trained about 70,000 students.
  3. It provides support exclusively to startups, not to academic institutions.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: C

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: C2S has placed EDA tools in 332 institutions and trained about 70,000 students. Statement 3 is wrong: the programme supports both academic institutions and startups - 105 startups have received EDA tool access alongside the institutions.

Source: PIB (MeitY, 6 Nov 2026) + Micron (28 Feb 2026) + Tata Electronics

6 Nov 2026 · Critical minerals auctions Economy

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to the Exploration Licence (EL) regime under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, consider the following statements:

  1. Exploration Licences were introduced through Section 10BA of the Act.
  2. They cover 29 critical and deep-seated minerals specified in the Seventh Schedule of the Act.
  3. An Exploration Licence entitles the holder to undertake mining operations in the licensed block.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: the EL regime came through Section 10BA and covers 29 critical and deep-seated minerals in the Seventh Schedule. Statement 3 is wrong: an EL permits only exploration (surveys, drilling, sampling), never mining.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Mines, 5 Nov 2026) + GMDC + Economic Geology 2022 for Gujarat items

Question 2

With reference to the auction of critical mineral blocks in India, consider the following statements:

  1. Mining lease and composite licence blocks are auctioned through a two-stage ascending forward auction, where the bidder quoting the highest percentage of revenue share wins.
  2. Exploration Licence blocks are auctioned through a two-stage descending reverse auction.
  3. Under both mechanisms, the winner is decided by the highest upfront payment to the government.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 match the notified process: forward ascending auctions for mining blocks (highest revenue-share wins) and reverse descending auctions for EL blocks (lowest share of the future lessee's revenue wins). Statement 3 is wrong: revenue share, not upfront payment, decides both.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Mines, 5 Nov 2026) + GMDC + Economic Geology 2022 for Gujarat items

Question 3

With reference to the mineral resources of Gujarat, consider the following statements:

  1. The Amba Dongar carbonatite complex in Chhota Udepur district is a documented rare earth element (REE) occurrence.
  2. Gujarat accounts for the largest share of rare earth element production in India.
  3. The Amba Dongar fluorspar mine in Gujarat is part of the mineral portfolio of a state public sector undertaking.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statement 1 is correct: Amba Dongar is a documented carbonatite-hosted REE occurrence. Statement 3 is correct: GMDC, the state PSU, lists the Ambadungar Fluorspar Mine (Kawant Taluka, Chhota Udepur district) in its mineral portfolio since 1963; the site is currently poised for operational revival. Statement 2 is wrong: Gujarat is not India's largest REE producer; the Amba Dongar REE is an occurrence, not production.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Mines, 5 Nov 2026) + GMDC + Economic Geology 2022 for Gujarat items

Question 4

With reference to the Ninth Tranche of critical and strategic mineral block auctions launched in November 2026, consider the following statements:

  1. Gujarat figures among the states offering critical mineral mining blocks in this tranche.
  2. Rajasthan's offerings in this tranche include potash and halite blocks.
  3. Tungsten blocks are among those offered in this tranche.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 2 and 3 are correct: Rajasthan offers potash-halite and tungsten blocks in Tranche IX. Statement 1 is wrong: Gujarat is not in the Tranche IX mining-block list at all. It appears only in the Exploration Licence tranche (one vanadium EL block), which is exploration, not mining.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Mines, 5 Nov 2026) + GMDC + Economic Geology 2022 for Gujarat items

Question 5

With reference to recent reforms to improve mineral auction outcomes in India, consider the following statements:

  1. The minimum number of technically qualified bidders required for the second round of auction was reduced from three to two for Part D minerals.
  2. Letters of Intent will now be issued automatically through the Unified Mining Portal.
  3. Insurance Surety Bonds have been prohibited in mineral auctions.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: the Mineral (Auction) Third Amendment Rules, 2026 (notified 16 October 2026) cut the second-round bidder minimum from three to two for Part D minerals, and Letters of Intent now go out automatically via the Unified Mining Portal. Statement 3 is wrong: Insurance Surety Bonds were introduced as an ease-of-doing-business reform, not prohibited.

Source: PIB (Ministry of Mines, 5 Nov 2026) + GMDC + Economic Geology 2022 for Gujarat items

5 Nov 2026 · Grid-scale energy storage Science & Technology

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to the Viability Gap Funding (VGF) scheme for grid-scale energy storage approved on 4 November 2026, consider the following statements:

  1. The scheme supports development of 92 GWh of storage capacity that is yet to be commissioned.
  2. Within the scheme, Hydro Pumped Storage has the largest share, at 60 GWh.
  3. The VGF outlay of Rs 14,500 crore equals the total estimated investment under the scheme.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are correct. PIB describes 92 GWh of yet-to-be-commissioned capacity, split 60 GWh PSP, 30 GWh BESS and 2 GWh alternative. Statement 3 is wrong: Rs 14,500 crore is the VGF support; the total estimated investment is Rs 97,600 crore.

Source: PIB

Question 2

With reference to energy storage units, consider the following statements:

  1. Power capacity is measured in megawatts (MW), while energy capacity is measured in megawatt-hours (MWh).
  2. A 100 MW / 400 MWh system can, under idealised assumptions, supply 100 MW for about four hours.
  3. Storage allows electricity generated at one time to be delivered when demand actually occurs.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: D

All three are correct. EIA defines power capacity in watts and energy capacity in watthours. Runtime equals usable energy divided by power under stated idealised assumptions. Time-shifting delivery is the core purpose of storage.

Source: EIA + DOE

Question 3

Consider the following statements about rechargeable batteries:

  1. Charge-discharge cycles reverse the battery's chemistry completely, so batteries do not degrade.
  2. During operation, electrons move through the external circuit while ions move through the electrolyte.
  3. A battery stores energy in the form of chemical potential.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 2 and 3 are correct. DOE describes chemical-potential storage with electrons in the external circuit and ions in the electrolyte. Statement 1 is wrong: the chemistry does not reverse completely, which reduces performance over time.

Source: DOE

Question 4

Consider the following statements about pumped storage hydropower:

  1. It stores and generates electricity by moving water between two reservoirs at different elevations.
  2. It produces net new energy after accounting for the electricity used in pumping.
  3. It requires suitable landscapes, lengthy permits and large initial capital.

How many of the above statements are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 3 are correct, so exactly two. Pumped storage consumes more energy charging than it returns on discharge; like all storage it operates with round-trip losses.

Source: DOE

Question 5

With reference to the design of the 4 November 2026 storage scheme, consider the following statements:

  1. Storage capacity will be allocated through a tariff-based competitive bidding process.
  2. Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) projects will be subject to a minimum Domestic Content Requirement of 40%.
  3. Only public-sector distribution companies are eligible for VGF support for BESS projects.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct. PIB states both public and private sector discoms are eligible for VGF support for BESS and Hydro PSP projects, so statement 3 is wrong.

Source: PIB

4 Nov 2026 · Aditya-L1 Science & Technology

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to Aditya-L1, consider the following statements:

  1. A halo orbit around Sun-Earth Lagrange point 1 (L1) offers continuous solar viewing.
  2. All seven payloads measure particles and fields locally near the spacecraft.
  3. The stated 1.5 million km distance of L1 is measured from Earth.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 3 are correct. ISRO states that four payloads remotely observe the Sun and three perform in-situ observations. The 1.5 million km is from Earth, not the Sun.

Source: ISRO

Question 2

A spectrograph records two branches of the same coronal emission line, one shifted to shorter and one to longer wavelengths. Which inference is most defensible?

Correct answer: C

Blueward and redward shifts supply opposite line-of-sight motion components. They do not alone establish a symmetric expansion, a common speed or a complete three-dimensional trajectory.

Source: PIB

Question 3

Consider the following statements about solar eruptions:

  1. A solar flare and a coronal mass ejection (CME) can occur together while remaining distinct phenomena.
  2. A CME is only a radiation burst with no plasma or magnetic field.
  3. Evidence of a CME is by itself sufficient to establish a new Earth-impact warning.

How many of the above statements are correct?

Correct answer: A

Only statement 1 is correct. NASA distinguishes a radiation burst from a CME cloud of plasma and magnetic field. The reported study reconstructs a 2024 event, not a new Earth-impact warning; observing an eruption alone does not establish such a warning.

Source: NASA + PIB

Question 4

Why did the Aditya-L1 study combine Visible Emission Line Coronagraph (VELC) spectroscopy, Atmospheric Imaging Assembly (AIA) imaging from the Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO), and a three-dimensional model?

Correct answer: D

VELC supplies line-of-sight information and AIA images supply plane-of-sky information. A geometric-kinematic model tests whether a proposed shape and expansion can reproduce their observed patterns. AIA imaging is not local particle sampling.

Source: PIB

Question 5

A symmetric-expansion model fails to reproduce an observed coronal mass ejection (CME) spectrum, while an inclined, asymmetrically expanding model gives a similar pattern. Consider the following inferences:

  1. The observations support asymmetric expansion for the studied event.
  2. The result establishes that every CME has the same asymmetric geometry.
  3. The result alone supplies an exact arrival time at Earth for future CMEs.

Which of the inferences given above is/are justified?

Correct answer: B

Only inference 1 is justified. A better fit supports that geometry for this studied event. It is neither a universal statement about all CMEs nor an exact operational forecast for future events.

Source: PIB
