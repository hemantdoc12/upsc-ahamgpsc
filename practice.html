11 Nov 2026 · Coal gasification scheme Economy

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to coal gasification, consider the following statements:

  1. Coal gasification produces synthesis gas, consisting mainly of carbon monoxide and hydrogen.
  2. Surface gasification converts coal into gas within the coal seam, without mining the coal.
  3. Synthesis gas can be used to produce ammonia, methanol and hydrogen.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 3 are correct: gasification yields syngas, mainly CO and H2, which is the feedstock for ammonia (and hence urea), methanol and hydrogen. Statement 2 is incorrect: in-seam conversion without mining describes underground coal gasification; surface gasification uses mined coal in a reactor.

Source: Established physical chemistry of gasification; the scheme's own title (surface projects)

Question 2

With reference to the Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects, consider the following statements:

  1. Round 2 of the scheme concluded with applications from five different companies.
  2. Round 3 of the scheme opened on 10 November 2026, with 11 January 2027 as the last date for submission of applications.
  3. The Round 2 applications envisage the production of urea, methanol and dimethyl ether.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: D

Statement 1 is incorrect: Round 2 concluded with two applications, both from NTPC Limited. Statements 2 and 3 are correct, per the Ministry of Coal's 10 November 2026 release: Round 3 runs from 10 November 2026 to 11 January 2027, and the NTPC applications envisage urea, methanol and dimethyl ether (DME).

Source: PIB, Ministry of Coal (10 Nov 2026), PRID 2334671

Question 3

Consider the following pairs:

  1. Coal gasification - conversion of coal into synthesis gas
  2. Coal liquefaction - conversion of coal into liquid hydrocarbon fuels
  3. Coal bed methane - methane produced by gasifying mined coal in surface reactors

How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

Correct answer: B

Pairs 1 and 2 are correctly matched. Pair 3 is wrong: coal bed methane is methane naturally adsorbed within coal seams, extracted by drilling; it is not a product of gasifying mined coal.

Source: Established distinctions: gasification vs liquefaction vs coal bed methane

Question 5

10 Nov 2026 · Unprorogued session, 88 days Polity

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to the summoning, adjournment and prorogation of Parliament, consider the following statements:

  1. Each House of Parliament is summoned to meet by the President.
  2. A House is adjourned sine die by the President of India.
  3. Prorogation is done by the President, normally on the advice of the Council of Ministers.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Under Article 85 the President summons each House and prorogues the Houses or either House, acting on ministerial advice under Article 74. Statement 2 is incorrect: adjournment, including sine die, is ordered by the presiding officer of the House, not the President.

Source: Constitution of India, Articles 85 and 74

Question 2

Consider the following statements about Article 85 of the Constitution:

  1. The maximum gap between the last sitting of a House in one session and its first sitting in the next session is six months.
  2. This six-month gap is counted from the date of prorogation of the previous session.
  3. The Constitution fixes a time limit within which the President must prorogue a House after it is adjourned sine die.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: C

Only statement 1 is correct. The six-month rule runs between sittings, not from prorogation, so statement 2 is wrong. And the Constitution sets no deadline for prorogation after a sine die adjournment; the practice of proroguing within days is convention, not constitutional text, so statement 3 is wrong.

Source: Constitution of India, Article 85(1); The Wire (9 Nov 2026)

Question 3

With reference to the fate of pending bills, consider the following statements:

  1. All bills pending in Parliament lapse when a session is prorogued.
  2. A bill that originated in the Rajya Sabha and is pending there does not lapse on the dissolution of the Lok Sabha.
  3. A bill passed by the Lok Sabha and pending in the Rajya Sabha lapses on the dissolution of the Lok Sabha.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 2 and 3 are correct. Prorogation lapses pending notices, not bills, so statement 1 is wrong. On dissolution of the Lok Sabha, a bill originating and pending in the Rajya Sabha survives, while a bill passed by the Lok Sabha but pending in the Rajya Sabha lapses.

Source: Established parliamentary practice: prorogation lapses notices, not bills

Question 4

With reference to the Rajya Sabha, consider the following statements:

  1. It is not subject to dissolution.
  2. It can be prorogued by the President even when the Lok Sabha is not prorogued.
  3. It is adjourned sine die by the President.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Article 83 makes the Rajya Sabha a permanent body, and Article 85(2)(a) lets the President prorogue the Houses or either House individually. Statement 3 is incorrect: the sine die adjournment of the Rajya Sabha is ordered by its Chairman, not the President.

Source: Constitution of India, Articles 83 and 85(2)(a)

Question 5

With reference to the Monsoon Session of 2026 remaining unprorogued for a record 88 days as of 9 November 2026, consider the following statements:

  1. The previous record of 87 unprorogued days, set in 2008, involved a reconvening of Parliament for a confidence motion.
  2. By not proroguing the session, the government has breached the six-month limit in Article 85.
  3. Because the session has not been prorogued, the Houses can be reconvened without a fresh summoning by the President.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 3 are correct. The 2008 session stayed unprorogued so Parliament could be recalled for the July 2008 confidence motion. Statement 2 is wrong: the six-month clock runs from the last sitting on 13 August 2026 regardless of prorogation, and it has not been breached. An unprorogued session can be reconvened without fresh summoning.

Source: PTI (9 Nov 2026); Reuters (22 Jul 2008); Article 85

9 Nov 2026 · Methane at COP31 Environment

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to the Global Methane Pledge (GMP), consider the following statements:

  1. It is a legally binding protocol under the UNFCCC that fixes country-specific methane caps.
  2. It aims at a collective reduction of methane emissions by at least 30% from 2020 levels by 2030.
  3. It was launched on the sidelines of COP26 in 2021.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 2 and 3 are correct. The GMP targets a collective cut of at least 30% from 2020 levels by 2030 and was launched on the sidelines of COP26 in 2021 by CCAC partners the US and the European Commission. Statement 1 is incorrect: the pledge is voluntary and sets a global, not national, target - there are no binding country caps.

Source: CCAC, Global Methane Pledge page

Question 2

With reference to methane as a greenhouse gas, consider the following statements:

  1. Its atmospheric lifetime is about 12 years.
  2. The waste sector accounts for roughly 20% of anthropogenic methane emissions.
  3. Over a 100-year horizon, methane traps about 86 times more heat than carbon dioxide per unit of mass.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: methane lasts about 12 years in the atmosphere, and waste contributes about 20% of human methane emissions (agriculture 40%, fossil fuels 35%). Statement 3 is a trap: the 86-times figure applies over 20 years; over 100 years methane is about 28 times stronger than CO2.

Source: CCAC, Methane (SLCP) page

Question 3

With reference to COP31 and its host country, consider the following statements:

  1. COP31 is being hosted by Türkiye at Antalya.
  2. Türkiye is listed among the participants of the Global Methane Pledge.
  3. Climate TRACE ranks Türkiye fifth in the world for methane emissions from solid waste.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: C

Statements 1 and 3 are correct: COP31 runs in Antalya, Türkiye (9-20 November 2026), and Climate TRACE places Türkiye fifth for solid-waste methane after China, the US, Russia and Brazil. Statement 2 is incorrect: as COP31 opened, Türkiye was not listed among GMP participants - a pointed gap for a host that made methane its priority.

Source: UNFCCC Media Bulletin Note 1 + The Hindu (Reuters, 7 Nov 2026)

Question 4

With reference to satellite monitoring of methane, consider the following statements:

  1. The UCLA analysis of Turkish waste sites drew on Carbon Mapper data from the Tanager-1 satellite.
  2. The Seymen landfill lies to the east of Istanbul.
  3. Methane's pungent odour makes landfill leaks easy to detect without instruments.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Only statement 1 is correct: UCLA researchers used public Carbon Mapper data from Tanager-1 (Jan 2025 - May 2026). Statement 2 is wrong: Seymen lies west of Istanbul. Statement 3 is wrong: methane is odourless - the landfill smell comes from other decomposing compounds, which is why satellites matter.

Source: The Hindu (Reuters, 7 Nov 2026)

Question 5

With reference to India and methane emissions, consider the following statements:

  1. India is a signatory to the Global Methane Pledge.
  2. Enteric fermentation in livestock and paddy cultivation are among India's major methane sources.
  3. The GOBAR-Dhan scheme incentivises the use of cattle waste for biogas production.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 2 and 3 are correct: India's methane comes mainly from livestock and paddy, and GOBAR-Dhan (with the National Biogas and Organic Manure Programme) promotes cattle-waste biogas. Statement 1 is incorrect: India has not signed the GMP, treating these as 'survival emissions' tied to food security and small farmers.

Source: Indian Express UPSC Essentials (29 May 2025)

8 Nov 2026 · E20 petrol and ethanol blending Economy

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) Programme, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol blending in petrol in India has increased from less than 1.5% in 2013-14 to 20% in 2025-26.
  2. India achieved its 20% blending target five years ahead of the schedule originally set for 2030.
  3. India's ethanol production capacity in 2026 is lower than it was in 2014.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: blending rose from under 1.5% (2013-14) to 20% (2025-26), and the 20% target (originally for 2030, later advanced to 2025-26) was met five years ahead of the original schedule. Statement 3 is wrong: capacity grew nearly fivefold, from 421 crore litres (2014) to about 2,000 crore litres (2026). [PIB Factsheet, 5 Jul 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG factsheet, 5 Jul 2026)

Question 2

With reference to ethanol production for the EBP Programme, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol is procured from sugarcane-based sources as well as grain-based sources such as maize and surplus rice.
  2. Only surplus rice, as determined and permitted by the Department of Food and Public Distribution after meeting national food security requirements, is diverted for ethanol production.
  3. Second-generation (2G) ethanol projects use lignocellulosic biomass such as agricultural residue as feedstock.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: D

All three are correct. The 1G supply base is sugarcane juice/molasses, maize and surplus rice; the DFPD permits only surplus rice after food-security needs are met; and 2G plants (supported under PM JI-VAN Yojana, Rs 1,969.50 crore for 12 projects, 2018-24) use lignocellulosic residue. [PIB Factsheet 5 Jul 2026; MoPNG 2G page]

Source: PIB (MoPNG factsheet, 5 Jul 2026) + MoPNG 2G ethanol page

Question 3

With reference to the fuel properties of ethanol and E20 petrol, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol has a research octane number (RON) of about 108.5, higher than that of petrol (about 84.4).
  2. Blending ethanol to E20 raises the effective octane rating of Indian petrol to around 95.
  3. Ethanol has a higher calorific value per litre than petrol, which increases the energy content of the blend.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct (RON ~108.5 vs ~84.4; E20 lifts effective octane to ~95). Statement 3 is wrong: ethanol's calorific value is about 30% LOWER than petrol's - the source of the mileage debate, though real-world service data (Maruti Suzuki) puts the drop at only ~0.6 km/l for a 20 km/l car. [PIB Factsheet, 5 Jul 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG factsheet, 5 Jul 2026)

Question 4

With reference to IS 17021, the BIS specification for ethanol-blended motor gasoline, consider the following statements:

  1. Ethanol content may be determined by Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) as per ASTM D5845.
  2. When Gas Chromatography under ASTM D4815/D6839 is used, the sample must first be diluted 1:1 with oxygenate-free petrol and the result corrected against an E10 certified reference material.
  3. Any private laboratory certificate mentioning the ethanol percentage is sufficient to establish non-conformity of a fuel sample under this standard.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 state the prescribed IS 17021:2018 (Amendment 3, Aug 2025) methods. Statement 3 is wrong: conformity must be established through the prescribed methods, with sampling under the MS/HSD Order 2005 and MDG 2024 (six one-litre samples, jointly signed labels, Schedule III laboratories) - a bare third-party certificate is a claim, not a finding. [PIB/MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026)

Question 5

With reference to the OMC/CHT Quality Coordination Cell (QCC) exercise reported in November 2026, consider the following statements:

  1. 893 E20 samples drawn from IOCL, BPCL and HPCL depots and retail outlets across 27 States and UTs were found within the prescribed 19-21% ethanol content range.
  2. All 56 samples tested in Karnataka recorded ethanol content between 19% and 20%.
  3. The exercise establishes that every retail outlet in India supplies fuel conforming to specifications.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are the reported QCC results (893 samples within 19-21%; Karnataka's 56 at 19-20%, average 19.30%). Statement 3 overreaches: a sampling exercise cannot certify every outlet, and the OMCs' own request for joint sampling and independent testing of the disputed Karnataka findings (26-31% claim) remains pending. [PIB/MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026]

Source: PIB (MoPNG, 7 Nov 2026)

With reference to the Exploration Licence (EL) regime under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, consider the following statements:

Question 2

With reference to the auction of critical mineral blocks in India, consider the following statements:

  1. Mining lease and composite licence blocks are auctioned through a two-stage ascending forward auction, where the bidder quoting the highest percentage of revenue share wins.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 match the notified process: forward ascending auctions for mining blocks (highest revenue-share wins) and reverse descending auctions for EL blocks (lowest share of the future lessee's revenue wins). Statement 3 is wrong: revenue share, not upfront payment, decides both.

Question 2

With reference to energy storage units, consider the following statements:

  1. Power capacity is measured in megawatts (MW), while energy capacity is measured in megawatt-hours (MWh).
  2. A 100 MW / 400 MWh system can, under idealised assumptions, supply 100 MW for about four hours.
  3. Storage allows electricity generated at one time to be delivered when demand actually occurs.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: D

All three are correct. EIA defines power capacity in watts and energy capacity in watthours. Runtime equals usable energy divided by power under stated idealised assumptions. Time-shifting delivery is the core purpose of storage.

Source: EIA + DOE

Question 3

Consider the following statements about rechargeable batteries:

  1. Charge-discharge cycles reverse the battery's chemistry completely, so batteries do not degrade.
  2. During operation, electrons move through the external circuit while ions move through the electrolyte.
  3. A battery stores energy in the form of chemical potential.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 2 and 3 are correct. DOE describes chemical-potential storage with electrons in the external circuit and ions in the electrolyte. Statement 1 is wrong: the chemistry does not reverse completely, which reduces performance over time.

Source: DOE

Question 4

Consider the following statements about pumped storage hydropower:

  1. It stores and generates electricity by moving water between two reservoirs at different elevations.
  2. It produces net new energy after accounting for the electricity used in pumping.
  3. It requires suitable landscapes, lengthy permits and large initial capital.

How many of the above statements are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 3 are correct, so exactly two. Pumped storage consumes more energy charging than it returns on discharge; like all storage it operates with round-trip losses.

Source: DOE

Question 5

With reference to the design of the 4 November 2026 storage scheme, consider the following statements:

  1. Storage capacity will be allocated through a tariff-based competitive bidding process.

  3. Only public-sector distribution companies are eligible for VGF support for BESS projects.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: A

Statements 1 and 2 are correct. PIB states both public and private sector discoms are eligible for VGF support for BESS and Hydro PSP projects, so statement 3 is wrong.

Source: PIB

4 Nov 2026 · Aditya-L1 Science & Technology

Read the lesson

Question 1

With reference to Aditya-L1, consider the following statements:

  1. A halo orbit around Sun-Earth Lagrange point 1 (L1) offers continuous solar viewing.
  2. All seven payloads measure particles and fields locally near the spacecraft.
  3. The stated 1.5 million km distance of L1 is measured from Earth.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 3 are correct. ISRO states that four payloads remotely observe the Sun and three perform in-situ observations. The 1.5 million km is from Earth, not the Sun.

Source: ISRO

Question 2

A spectrograph records two branches of the same coronal emission line, one shifted to shorter and one to longer wavelengths. Which inference is most defensible?

Correct answer: C

Blueward and redward shifts supply opposite line-of-sight motion components. They do not alone establish a symmetric expansion, a common speed or a complete three-dimensional trajectory.

Source: PIB

Question 3

Consider the following statements about solar eruptions:

  1. A solar flare and a coronal mass ejection (CME) can occur together while remaining distinct phenomena.
  2. A CME is only a radiation burst with no plasma or magnetic field.
  3. Evidence of a CME is by itself sufficient to establish a new Earth-impact warning.

How many of the above statements are correct?

Correct answer: A

Only statement 1 is correct. NASA distinguishes a radiation burst from a CME cloud of plasma and magnetic field. The reported study reconstructs a 2024 event, not a new Earth-impact warning; observing an eruption alone does not establish such a warning.

Source: NASA + PIB

Question 4

Why did the Aditya-L1 study combine Visible Emission Line Coronagraph (VELC) spectroscopy, Atmospheric Imaging Assembly (AIA) imaging from the Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO), and a three-dimensional model?

Correct answer: D

VELC supplies line-of-sight information and AIA images supply plane-of-sky information. A geometric-kinematic model tests whether a proposed shape and expansion can reproduce their observed patterns. AIA imaging is not local particle sampling.

Source: PIB

Question 5

A symmetric-expansion model fails to reproduce an observed coronal mass ejection (CME) spectrum, while an inclined, asymmetrically expanding model gives a similar pattern. Consider the following inferences:
