One news item, fifteen exam angles · 12 Nov 2026 · about eight minutes

Rooms for a civilisation: the Rs 3,230 crore Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum

Less to read. More to connect. The Cabinet has approved the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum at the North and South Block for Rs 3,230 crore - the old Secretariat becoming what the government says will be the museum with the world's largest display area. The buildings, the board that appraised the project, and the museums it will be measured against.

Two PIBs, not one. The Public Investment Board appraised this project; the Press Information Bureau announced it. The acronym is the exam.

Fifteen connected angles

01 · News

The approval

On 11 November 2026 the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs, chaired by the Prime Minister, approved the development of the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum at the North and South Block complex in New Delhi, at an estimated total project cost of Rs 3,230 crore.

02 · News

What is being built

A landmark civilizational museum presenting the continuity, diversity and richness of India's tangible and intangible heritage through curated thematic galleries, immersive technologies and visitor-centric interpretation, over a proposed built-up area of about 1,78,509 square metres.

03 · History

The buildings themselves

North Block and South Block, the twin Secretariat buildings, were designed by Herbert Baker as part of Lutyens' New Delhi, inaugurated as India's capital in 1931. They currently house the Home, Finance, External Affairs and Defence ministries and the Prime Minister's Office, which are relocating to new office complexes.

04 · Concept

Adaptive reuse

Adaptive reuse gives a heritage building a new function while keeping its architectural character. The museum project preserves the facades, courtyards, corridors and architectural integrity of the two blocks with minimal intervention, converting rooms into gallery and display areas.

05 · Polity

How big projects get cleared

Large Central sector projects are appraised by the Public Investment Board (which considers Central sector projects above Rs 500 crore) before they go to the Cabinet or its committees. This proposal was appraised on 27 July 2026, the Ministry of Culture concurred with the Board's recommendations, and the CCEA, chaired by the Prime Minister, gave the final approval.

06 · Polity

Two PIBs, one acronym

The Public Investment Board appraises public investment projects and is headed by the Secretary, Department of Expenditure. The Press Information Bureau publicises government policy. Same initials, entirely different jobs; the museum file passed through the first and was announced by the second.

07 · Culture

The scale claim

The government says the museum is planned to have the world's largest display area, more than 1 lakh square metres: bigger than the Louvre in Paris (73,000), the British Museum in London (70,000) and the Hermitage in St. Petersburg (66,800). Display area is the floor space where objects are actually shown, not a building's total size.

08 · Culture

The collection

Around 1.8 million objects have been identified for the museum from the National Museum, other Ministry of Culture museums, 52 Archaeological Survey of India site museums, state museums and private collections.

09 · Culture

The galleries

The first inaugural gallery, on the theme of Adornment, is expected to open in the North Block in the later part of 2027. Other themes being developed include Architecture and Cityscapes, Mathematics and Astronomy, Languages and Scripts, Faith and Spirituality, and Bharat Connects - Trade and Ideas.

10 · History

The timeline

The Prime Minister announced the museum on 18 May 2023. Implementation is phased over FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31, with a modular approach to add galleries over time.

11 · Polity

Why the buildings are free

The Central Vista redevelopment is moving ministries out of the colonial-era Secretariat into new common office complexes. The release describes the relocation of government offices as the opportunity for adaptive reuse of North and South Block for a public cultural purpose.

12 · Culture

India's museum map

The Indian Museum in Kolkata, founded in 1814 by the Asiatic Society of Bengal, is the oldest and largest museum in India. The National Museum in New Delhi, established in 1949, functions under the Ministry of Culture. The Salar Jung Museum in Hyderabad is known for one of the largest one-man collections of art and artefacts in the world.

13 · Concept

Museums as institutions

A museum is not a warehouse of objects: it conserves, researches and interprets. The project includes collection storage and conservation facilities, research and education roles, and museology work spanning artisans, craftspeople, designers, performers and technology providers.

14 · Economy

The museum economy

The museum is being equipped to handle one crore visitors a year (the deck plans for 30,000 a day with timed entry) and is expected to generate recurring revenue through ticket sales, food and beverages, events and venue rentals, digital experiences, guided tours and retail, alongside direct and indirect employment.

15 · International

Soft power

The release frames the museum as a cultural destination strengthening India's global cultural presence and soft power. National museums are instruments of cultural diplomacy: the Louvre, the British Museum and the Hermitage are as much statements of national identity as they are collections.

Question 1

With reference to the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum, consider the following statements:

  1. It is being developed through the adaptive reuse of the North Block and South Block buildings in New Delhi.
  2. The project was appraised by the Public Investment Board before Cabinet approval.
  3. It is being developed by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: B

Statements 1 and 2 are correct: the museum uses adaptive reuse of the North and South Block complex, and the proposal was appraised by the Public Investment Board on 27 July 2026 before the CCEA approved it. Statement 3 is incorrect: the project is being developed by the Ministry of Culture.

Source: PIB (CCEA), 11 Nov 2026, PRID 2334961

Question 2

With reference to the architecture of New Delhi, consider the following statements:

  1. The North Block and the South Block were designed by Herbert Baker.
  2. Edwin Lutyens alone designed the old Parliament House building.
  3. The Rashtrapati Bhavan was designed by Herbert Baker.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: A

Only statement 1 is correct: Herbert Baker designed the twin Secretariat buildings, North Block and South Block. Statement 2 is incorrect: the old Parliament House was designed jointly by Edwin Lutyens and Herbert Baker. Statement 3 is incorrect: the Rashtrapati Bhavan (the former Viceroy's House) was designed by Edwin Lutyens.

Source: Established architectural history of New Delhi: Baker designed the Secretariat buildings and, jointly with Lutyens, the old Parliament House; Lutyens designed the Rashtrapati Bhavan

Question 3

With reference to the approval of major public investment projects in India, consider the following statements:

  1. The Public Investment Board appraises major Central sector projects before they are placed before the Cabinet or its committees.
  2. The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs is chaired by the Union Finance Minister.
  3. The Public Investment Board is headed by the Secretary, Department of Expenditure.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: C

Statements 1 and 3 are correct: the Public Investment Board, headed by the Secretary, Department of Expenditure, appraises major Central sector projects (above Rs 500 crore) before Cabinet-level consideration; the museum proposal was appraised on 27 July 2026. Statement 2 is incorrect: the CCEA is chaired by the Prime Minister.

Source: PIB (CCEA), 11 Nov 2026, PRID 2334961; established institutional roles of the Public Investment Board and the CCEA

Question 4

With reference to museums in India, consider the following statements:

  1. The Indian Museum in Kolkata, established in 1814, is the oldest and largest museum in India.
  2. The Salar Jung Museum in Hyderabad is known for one of the largest one-man collections of art and artefacts in the world.
  3. The National Museum in New Delhi functions under the Ministry of Culture.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

Correct answer: D

All three statements are correct. The Indian Museum, Kolkata (1814, founded by the Asiatic Society of Bengal) is India's oldest and largest museum. The Salar Jung Museum in Hyderabad is famous for the single-collector collection of Salar Jung III. The National Museum, New Delhi, established in 1949, is a subordinate office under the Ministry of Culture.

Source: Established museum records: Indian Museum (1814), Salar Jung Museum, National Museum (Ministry of Culture)

Question 5

Consider the following pairs:

  1. Louvre - Paris
  2. Hermitage - St. Petersburg
  3. British Museum - New York

How many of the above pairs are correctly matched?

Correct answer: B

Only two pairs are correctly matched. The Louvre is in Paris and the Hermitage is in St. Petersburg; both figure in the government's comparison of museum display areas (73,000 and 66,800 square metres respectively). The British Museum is in London, not New York, with a display area of about 70,000 square metres.

Source: PIB Cabinet briefing deck, 11 Nov 2026 (display-area figures); established museum locations
