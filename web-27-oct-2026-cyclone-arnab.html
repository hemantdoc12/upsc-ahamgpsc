The storm today

Very severe cyclonic storm Arnab is over the west-central Bay of Bengal and is forecast to cross the Andhra Pradesh-Yanam coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam, close to Yanam, by the forenoon of 28 Oct (IMD).

Where it stands

Arnab is now a very severe cyclonic storm. At 0230 IST on 27 October it lay over the west-central Bay of Bengal near 15.9 N / 85.4 E, about 300 km southeast of Visakhapatnam, moving west-northwestwards at 25 kmph. It is likely to turn northwestwards from the evening of 27 October and cross close to Yanam by the forenoon of 28 October (IMD Bulletin 15).

After the landfall

Once it crosses, it is likely to affect districts in Telangana, southern Odisha, south and central Chhattisgarh, apart from eastern Uttar Pradesh and Bihar. Fishermen have been advised not to venture into the sea from 26 to 29 October; those already at sea have returned (PIB).

The response table

The Cabinet Secretary chaired the NCMC review with the Home Secretary, NDMA, IMD, NDRF, the Coast Guard and officials of four states and Puducherry. NDRF teams are deployed with more on standby; Army, Navy, Air Force and Coast Guard rescue teams are ready; the objective stated is zero loss of life (PIB).

With reference to cyclonic storm Arnab, consider the following statements:

  1. Its name was contributed by Bangladesh to the WMO/ESCAP panel list.
  2. The National Crisis Management Committee is chaired by the Union Home Secretary.
  3. It is forecast to cross the coast between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam.
  4. RSMC New Delhi names tropical cyclones over the north Indian Ocean.

Answer: (b) 1, 3 and 4 only

(b) is correct: the name Arnab was contributed by Bangladesh to the WMO/ESCAP panel list (IMD/RSMC New Delhi); the forecast crossing is between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam (IMD Bulletin 15); and RSMC New Delhi names tropical cyclones over the north Indian Ocean (IMD).

(a) misses true statement 4: RSMC New Delhi’s naming role.

(c) includes false statement 2: the NCMC is chaired by the Cabinet Secretary, not the Union Home Secretary (the Home Secretary chairs the National Executive Committee).
