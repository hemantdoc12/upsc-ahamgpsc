What happened

Primary-source checked

The milestone

India completed its target of 11 crore Farmer IDs by the night of 23 Oct 2026, five months ahead of the 31 Mar 2027 deadline, under the Digital Agriculture Mission (PIB).

What the Farmer ID changes

The release frames the Farmer ID as a single verified identity for farm services: KCC and bank loans with less repeated document verification, PMFBY claims assessed and paid more transparently into bank accounts, faster disaster relief to genuine and eligible farmers, and MSP procurement with fewer intermediaries. These are stated expectations, not measured outcomes.

The machinery behind it

The Digital Agriculture Mission was launched after Union Cabinet approval on 2 September 2024. Its digital public infrastructure links a verified farmer database with services; the Ministry says over 6 crore farmers have already received direct benefits through Farmer ID-based delivery, with duplication curbed and monitoring improved.

Federalism and inclusion

The Centre credited Centre-state-field coordination for finishing five months early, calling it cooperative federalism. States and UTs are taking steps, under applicable rules, to bring tenant farmers and actual cultivators onto the platform. Land records remain a State subject (static: check).

The hub: one verified farmer identity ties credit, insurance, procurement and relief to a single registry.

    Less paperwork can still hide new gatekeepers. What makes digital delivery genuinely simpler for the last farmer?

    Land is a state subject, yet a national target was met early through district teams. Is field-level federalism India's real DPI?

    Land records name owners; tenants till the soil. Whose name should anchor farm welfare when the two differ?

    A verified database promises fewer leakages on paper. Does verification change farm outcomes, or only audits?

Today's MCQ: answer and why

One Prelims-style question on today's item. This is the only drafted question on the page. The carousel slide shows the question. The key lives here.

With reference to the Farmer ID initiative under the Digital Agriculture Mission, consider the following statements:

  1. The target of creating 11 crore Farmer IDs was completed ahead of the 31 March 2027 deadline.
  2. The Digital Agriculture Mission was approved by the Union Cabinet in September 2024.
  3. Land records fall in the Union List of the Seventh Schedule.
  4. The Farmer ID is a statutory identity created under the Aadhaar Act, 2016.

Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only

(a) is correct: the 11 crore target was completed ahead of the 31 March 2027 deadline, and the Digital Agriculture Mission was approved by the Union Cabinet in September 2024 (PIB).

(b) includes false statement 4: the Farmer ID is a registry identity under the Mission, not a statutory identity under the Aadhaar Act, 2016.

(c) includes false statement 3: land records are a State subject under the Seventh Schedule, not a Union List subject (static: check).

(d) includes both false statements 3 and 4. Sources: PIB release of 24 October 2026; Seventh Schedule detail is static knowledge (check).
